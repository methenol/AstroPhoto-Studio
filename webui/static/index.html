Pipeline

  1. Calibratebias, dark & flat masters (a DWARF's CALI_FRAME, darks/ flats/ biases/); dark scaled per sub to its sensor temperature; flat checked against the sky
  2. Analyse framesstars, FWHM, roundness and background of every sub (Siril registration); weak frames are rejected
  3. Register & integratetwo-pass registration, Bayer drizzle, rejection stacking, auto-crop, plate solve (Siril)
  4. Restoregradient removal, colour calibration (SPCC), deconvolution and noise reduction on the linear stack; BlurXTerminator / NoiseXTerminator when licensed, Cosmic Clarity when chosen
  5. Remove starsStarXTerminator when licensed, else StarNet2 (Cosmic Clarity DarkStar when chosen under Stars): separates the stars so the Star reduction slider can go all the way to starless
  6. Auto-finishsets the processing settings on the right from measurements of the image
